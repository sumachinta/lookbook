Canvas Snap Tester

Drop your Gemini-beautified catalog images here to see the free background snap to #f8f8f5 — and which images it can't handle cleanly.

This runs the exact algorithm proposed for the app: sample the corner background, flood-fill inward from the edges within a tolerance, repaint that region to #f8f8f5. Interior pixels (buttons, light panels) are never touched because they aren't connected to the edge. Use the tolerance slider to find a value that snaps the background without eating the item, then watch the verdict badges — anything marked needs real removal is a candidate for the Cloudinary fallback later.
